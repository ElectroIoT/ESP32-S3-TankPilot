Board size: 94 x 70 mm

2 layers, 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz copper, 94 x 70 mm, 3 mm corners, three M3 holes (3.2 mm, non-plated, no pad). Parts add up to about 2908 mm² of 6580 mm² (44 %). The size is set by the optional 230 V block: the HLK-5M05 module alone is 38 x 23 mm and needs the fuse, varistor, AC terminal and a 6.4 mm slot. Without the HLK block the board shrinks to about 72 x 70 mm (nothing right of x 72 mm is needed). Both stay under 100 x 100 mm, the cheapest PCB price tier, and the full board fits the enclosure below.

Enclosure: 120 x 80 x 55 mm IP67 transparent ABS box (IDIS SKU 10188)

Fit check. The data sheet gives 120 x 80 mm outside and about 115 x 75 mm inside, but the four lid-screw posts (about 11.5 mm square) and two mid-side bosses (105 mm apart) reduce the free floor to about 96 x 75 mm. The board is 94 x 70 mm, so it fits with about 1 mm to the bosses and 2.5 mm top and bottom, and stays well under the 100 x 100 mm limit. A 100 mm wide board would NOT fit. Height inside is about 43 mm: the tallest parts (470 uF C1 about 12 mm, HLK module 18 mm, screw terminals about 10 mm) are fine.
  • Mounting: the box has no documented PCB posts, so use 3 M3 standoffs on adhesive pads or a small mounting plate through the three holes of the board; confirm the real posts on the box before ordering the PCB.
  • Cable entries (IP67): drill the box for cable glands (PG7 / PG9): 5 V, ultrasonic, float, probes (one gland for the 6 wires), LDR and DHT11 cable. Keep the glands on the bottom edge next to the screw terminals J4, J8, J10, J11. Because the box is sealed, use the J10 screw terminal for the 5 V supply; the DC jack J1 and the USB-C J5 are only for use with the lid open.
  • 230 V option: only with an engineer review; keep the mains gland and wiring away from the probe cables, and note the clear lid shows everything.
  • The drawing file (.dwg, AutoCAD 2018) could not be opened on this PC; the numbers above come from the data-sheet image (120 / 80 / 55 mm, holes 111.5 x 71.5 mm, 105 mm between inner bosses, 43 mm inside height). Export the DWG as PDF or DXF if you want the exact inner shape checked.

Edge plan

  • Top edge: J2 (ultrasonic), J3 (float), J12 (DHT11), J6 (expansion), antenna end of the ESP32-C3 module.
  • Left edge: J1 (DC jack) and J5 (USB-C).
  • Bottom edge: J4 and J8 (probe terminals, cables down).
  • Right side: J10 (5 V terminal) and J11 (LDR) in the free low-voltage area, then the optional 230 V block with J9 (AC in) on the far right.

Silkscreen box per block: one dashed rectangle with its title on the Top Silkscreen layer (as in the BorePilot guide). The checker confirmed: no overlaps, no part outside its own box, no box touching a mounting hole or another box, 3.2 mm from low-voltage parts to the mains parts, and nothing above the antenna end.

Order of work

  1. In EasyEDA PCB draw the outline 94 x 70 mm, 3 mm corners, three 3.2 mm non-plated holes at (4,4), (4,66), (90,66) mm, and (if you fit the HLK block) the slot x 72.4 to 73.7 mm, y 1.5 to 67 mm.
  2. Import the schematic (Design > Update/Convert Schematic to PCB). Check every footprint against its datasheet first.
  3. Place block by block with the X / Y tables, ticking each part here.
  4. Draw one rectangle per block on the Top Silkscreen layer with its title (the dashed boxes).
  5. Draw the net classes from the table above, then pour GND on the low-voltage side.
  6. Route the HLK block (if fitted) and the USB pair first, then power, then sensors.
  7. Run DRC with a 6.4 mm clearance rule between mains and low-voltage nets. Add silkscreen: 5 V DC ONLY, MAINS WARNING, terminal pin names (COM, 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, FULL, F, GND, 5V, TRIG, ECHO).
  8. Export Gerber and have the PCB maker check the slot and the spacing.
