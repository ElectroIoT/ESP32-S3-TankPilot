TankPilot - schematic build guide (EasyEDA Pro web)

Draw the schematic by hand, page by page: parts to search by LCSC number, wiring steps in order, net names, checks. Progress is saved in this browser.

Plan stage. Planned design, not a finished schematic or PCB: nets come from PLAN.md, pin names and footprints must be checked in EasyEDA and against datasheets. The optional HLK 230 V block involves mains voltage: engineer review before any mains power; use the 5 V adapter or USB if unsure.

0. Setup

1. Open EasyEDA Pro (web), new project TankPilot with one schematic and one PCB.
2. Make 3 schematic pages named as above (page names 1_POWER_USB, 2_MCU, 3_SENSORS).
3. Place parts by searching the LCSC number (C#) in the library search; set the Designator to the Ref in the table.
4. Use net labels for every connection that leaves a page; use the same name on both pages. Names are case-sensitive.
5. Mark optional parts (HLK block, ESD U5-U7, J6) with the Not-in-BOM attribute when you do not fit them.
6. Run the schematic check after each page and fix every warning.

2. Wire in this order

1. J1 DC jack: pin 1 (tip, +) to net DC_IN, pin 2 (sleeve, -) to GND. Add F1 (PTC) from DC_IN to DC_F, D1 TVS from DC_F to GND (cathode at DC_F). J10 (2-pin screw terminal, +5 V and GND) joins the same nets as J1: pin 1 to DC_IN, pin 2 to GND; use J1 or J10, not both at once.
2. D2: anode DC_F, cathode +5V. D3: anode VBUS, cathode +5V. C1 470 uF (+ to +5V), C2 and C5 on +5V to GND.
3. U2 AP2112K: VIN and EN to +5V, GND to GND, VOUT to +3V3; C3 10 uF on +3V3. Check the symbol pin order (VIN 1, GND 2, EN 3, NC 4, VOUT 5).
4. OPTIONAL HLK block: J9 L and N to nets AC_L and AC_N; F2 from AC_L to AC_LF (F2H = its two clips); RV1 across AC_LF and AC_N; U8 AC pins to AC_LF / AC_N, +Vo to +5V_HLK, -Vo to GND; D4 from +5V_HLK to +5V; C6 on +5V_HLK. Draw a MAINS WARNING box around AC_L / AC_N / AC_LF.
5. J5 USB-C: VBUS pins (A4B9, B4A9) to VBUS, GND pins and EH1-4 to GND, A5 CC1 to R1 (5.1 k) to GND, B5 CC2 to R2 to GND, SBU1 / SBU2 no-connect.
6. D+ pins A6 and B6 joined = USB_DP_J, D- pins A7 and B7 joined = USB_DN_J. U3 USBLC6: pin 1 USB_DP_J, pin 6 USB_DP, pin 3 USB_DN_J, pin 4 USB_DN, pin 5 VBUS, pin 2 GND. C7 on VBUS.

3. Check before leaving the page

  • Nets leaving this page: +5V, +3V3, VBUS, GND, USB_DP, USB_DN, +5V_HLK. AC_L / AC_N / AC_LF stay on this page only.
  • Diode orientation: the bar (cathode) is on the +5V side of D2, D3, D4 and at DC_F for D1.

2. Wire in this order

1. Place U1 ESP32-C3-MINI-1-N4 (C2838502). Pins 1, 2, 11, 14 and the pad pins 36-53 to GND, pin 3 to +3V3. Add C4 10 uF and C9, C10 100 nF on +3V3.
2. EN (pin 8): R3 10 k to +3V3, C8 100 nF to GND, SW1 to GND. BOOT: SW2 from net BOOT (pin 23, GPIO9) to GND. Tact switch pins 1+2 are joined, 3+4 are joined: use pin 1 and pin 3.
3. USB: pin 26 (IO18) to USB_DN, pin 27 (IO19) to USB_DP (page 1).
4. Net labels on the sensor pins: pin 12 IO0 PROBE_20, pin 13 IO1 PROBE_40, pin 6 IO3 PROBE_60, pin 19 IO5 PROBE_80, pin 20 IO6 PROBE_FULL, pin 18 IO4 PROBE_COM, pin 21 IO7 FLOAT1, pin 16 IO10 DHT, pin 5 IO2 LDR, pin 30 (IO20) US_TRIG, pin 31 (IO21) US_ECHO.
5. LED_ST: pin 22 (IO8) to R7 1 k to LED2 (green) to GND. LED1 (red) with R6 1 k from +3V3 to GND (power LED). LDR: pin 5 (IO2) also to J6 pin 4; J6 pins 1 +5V, 2 +3V3, 3 GND (not fitted by default).
6. Leave the NC pins (4, 7, 9, 10, 15, 17, 24, 25, 28, 29, 32-35) unconnected with No-Connect flags. Nothing except SW2 on GPIO9.

3. Check before leaving the page

  • Every GPIO net label here must appear again on page 3 (or page 1 for USB). Single-use labels are typos.
  • Antenna end of the module: nothing above it on the PCB (see the layout guide).

2. Wire in this order

1. Ultrasonic J2: pin 1 +5V (C14 100 nF to GND), pin 2 TRIG to net US_TRIG, pin 3 ECHO to net ECHO_5V, pin 4 GND. R15 1 k from ECHO_5V to US_ECHO, R16 2 k from US_ECHO to GND (5 V to 3.3 V divider).
2. Float J3: pin 1 to FLOAT1_T, pin 2 to GND. R8 1 k from FLOAT1_T to FLOAT1; R4 10 k from +3V3 to FLOAT1; C12 100 nF from FLOAT1 to GND. U4 USBLC6: pins 1+6 FLOAT1_T, pins 3 and 4 no-connect, pin 5 +3V3, pin 2 GND.
3. Probes J4 + J8: J4 pin 1 COM = PROBE_COM_T, pin 2 = PROBE_20_T, pin 3 = PROBE_40_T; J8 pin 1 = PROBE_60_T, pin 2 = PROBE_80_T, pin 3 = PROBE_FULL_T. C11 1 uF from PROBE_COM to PROBE_COM_T (blocks DC).
4. Each probe line: 1 k series (R10 to R14) from PROBE_xx_T to PROBE_xx, then 470 k (R17 to R21) from PROBE_xx to GND. OPTIONAL ESD U5 (COM + 20 %), U6 (40 % + 60 %), U7 (80 % + FULL): terminal-side nets on pins 1+6 and 3+4, pin 5 +3V3, pin 2 GND.
5. LDR J11 (2-pin terminal for an LDR on a cable): pin 1 = LDR_T, pin 2 = GND. R24 1 k from LDR_T to net LDR; R22 10 k from +3V3 to LDR; C15 100 nF from LDR to GND. LDR goes to GPIO2 (ADC1). Dark = high voltage, bright = low (invert in firmware). GPIO2 is a strapping pin: bench check that bright light at reset does not stop the boot (unverified).
6. DHT11 J12 (1x3 header): pin 1 +3V3, pin 2 DATA = net DHT (to GPIO10), pin 3 GND. R23 10 k from +3V3 to DHT. Check the pin order of your DHT11 board: bare sensors are VCC, DATA, NC, GND; 3-pin modules are often DATA, VCC, GND or VCC, DATA, GND.

3. Check before leaving the page

  • Terminal-side nets end in _T so the pull-ups and series resistors sit between the cable and the GPIO.
  • The probe circuit is an untested design idea: keep the resistor footprints so values can be changed on the bench.

Final checks

1. Every net label is used at least twice. One GND, one +5V, one +3V3. AC_* nets exist only if you fit the HLK block.
2. Compare the part count with the BOM page (68 pieces, optional parts included).
3. Compare every footprint with its datasheet before the layout: C165948 USB-C, DC-005 jack (centre pin), ESP32-C3-MINI-1, KF128 terminals, SOT-25 LDO.
4. Export the schematic PDF and send it for a check.
