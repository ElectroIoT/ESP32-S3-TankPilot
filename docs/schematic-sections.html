Setup in EasyEDA

1. Open EasyEDA Pro (web), new project TankPilot with one schematic and one PCB.
2. Make 3 schematic sheets named as above (page names 1_POWER_USB, 2_MCU, 3_SENSORS).
3. Place parts by searching the LCSC number (C#) in the library search; set the Designator to the Ref in the table.
4. Use net labels for every connection that leaves a page; use the same name on both pages. Names are case-sensitive.
5. Mark optional parts (HLK block, ESD U5-U7, J6) with the Not-in-BOM attribute when you do not fit them.
6. Run the schematic check after each page and fix every warning.

Build steps (tick as you draw this sheet)
1. J1 DC jack: pin 1 (tip, +) to net DC_IN, pin 2 (sleeve, -) to GND. Add F1 (PTC) from DC_IN to DC_F, D1 TVS from DC_F to GND (cathode at DC_F). J10 (2-pin screw terminal, +5 V and GND) joins the same nets as J1: pin 1 to DC_IN, pin 2 to GND; use J1 or J10, not both at once.
2. D2: anode DC_F, cathode +5V. D3: anode VBUS, cathode +5V. C1 470 uF (+ to +5V), C2 and C5 on +5V to GND.
3. U2 AP2112K: VIN and EN to +5V, GND to GND, VOUT to +3V3; C3 10 uF on +3V3. Check the symbol pin order (VIN 1, GND 2, EN 3, NC 4, VOUT 5).
4. OPTIONAL HLK block: J9 L and N to nets AC_L and AC_N; F2 from AC_L to AC_LF (F2H = its two clips); RV1 across AC_LF and AC_N; U8 AC pins to AC_LF / AC_N, +Vo to +5V_HLK, -Vo to GND; D4 from +5V_HLK to +5V; C6 on +5V_HLK. Draw a MAINS WARNING box around AC_L / AC_N / AC_LF.
5. J5 USB-C: VBUS pins (A4B9, B4A9) to VBUS, GND pins and EH1-4 to GND, A5 CC1 to R1 (5.1 k) to GND, B5 CC2 to R2 to GND, SBU1 / SBU2 no-connect.
6. D+ pins A6 and B6 joined = USB_DP_J, D- pins A7 and B7 joined = USB_DN_J. U3 USBLC6: pin 1 USB_DP_J, pin 6 USB_DP, pin 3 USB_DN_J, pin 4 USB_DN, pin 5 VBUS, pin 2 GND. C7 on VBUS.

GPIO2 (pin 5) is a strapping pin and must be high at reset (data sheet table 6), so it carries the DHT11 line, which idles high through R23. The LDR is on GPIO0 (pin 12), which is not a strapping pin. GPIO8 (pin 22, status LED) and GPIO9 (pin 23, BOOT) are the other strapping pins: for USB download mode hold SW2 (BOOT) while pressing SW1 (EN).

Pin guide: USBLC6-2SC6 (C7519) for U3, U4, U5, U6, U7

SOT-23-6 has 3 pins on each side. Top view: pins 1, 2, 3 run down the left side, pins 4, 5, 6 run up the right side (pin 6 top right, pin 4 bottom right); the dot marks pin 1. Pin meaning (data sheet): 1 = I/O1, 2 = GND, 3 = I/O2, 4 = I/O2, 5 = VBUS (supply side), 6 = I/O1. Pins 1 and 6 are the same channel (joined inside), and so are pins 3 and 4.

The data line goes IN on pin 1 and OUT on pin 6 (D+), IN on pin 3 and OUT on pin 4 (D-). Do not join pin 1 to pin 6 with a wire: the two pins are joined inside the chip.

Used only as a clamp: pins 1 and 6 on the same net. The second channel (pins 3, 4) is not used: No-Connect flags.

Optional part. Channel 1 = pins 1 and 6 (COM), channel 2 = pins 3 and 4 (20 %).

Final checks

1. Every net label is used at least twice. One GND, one +5V, one +3V3. AC_* nets exist only if you fit the HLK block.
2. Compare the part count with the BOM page (68 pieces, optional parts included).
3. Compare every footprint with its datasheet before the layout: C165948 USB-C, DC-005 jack (centre pin), ESP32-C3-MINI-1, KF128 terminals, SOT-25 LDO.
4. Export the schematic PDF and send it for a check.
