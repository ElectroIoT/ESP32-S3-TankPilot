External wiring (what you connect by hand)

ConnectionHow
5 V in (J1, J10 or J5)Regulated 5 V only. Use one of: J1 DC jack 5.5 / 2.1 mm (centre +), J10 2-pin screw terminal (pin 1 = +5 V, pin 2 = GND) or USB-C J5. The PTC F1 limits to about 0.5 A on J1 / J10. Never 12 V.
Optional mains (J9, HLK block)L and N from a breaker, strain-relieved, in an IP65 box, away from the probe wiring. Leave the whole HLK block off unless an engineer has reviewed it. Mains is near water here.
Ultrasonic (J2)Pin 1 +5 V, pin 2 TRIG, pin 3 ECHO, pin 4 GND (JSN-SR04T: match your module's labels). ECHO is divided to 3.3 V on the board.
Float switch (J3)Two wires, either way round, dry contact only (never mains or the pump contactor on the same wires). One float input only.
Probes (J4 + J8)J4: 1 COM, 2 = 20 %, 3 = 40 %. J8: 1 = 60 %, 2 = 80 %, 3 = FULL. With the 5-probe kit use COM + 4 levels and leave one terminal empty. Wire COM to the longest probe.
LDR (J11)Two wires to an LDR, either way round, up to a few metres of cable.
DHT11 (J12)Pin 1 +3V3, pin 2 DATA, pin 3 GND. Check your DHT11 board's pin order first.
Expansion (J6, not fitted)Pin 1 +5 V, pin 2 +3V3, pin 3 GND, pin 4 IO2 (shared with the LDR input).
USB-C (J5)Programming, logs and 5 V. Native USB, no USB-UART chip: hold BOOT while plugging in if the first flash fails.

Sheet 1: Power (DC jack, 5 V terminal, USB-C, HLK option)

5 V POWER (DC jack, USB, 3.3 V)

5 V from the DC jack J1 or the 2-pin screw terminal J10 (both through the PTC fuse + TVS), or USB-C, or the optional HLK module, OR-ed with Schottky diodes, then an AP2112K 3.3 V LDO.

HLK 230 V -> 5 V (OPTIONAL)

Optional mains supply: AC in terminal, fuse, varistor and the HLK-5M05 module. Leave all of these parts off if you power the board from a 5 V adapter.

USB-C PROGRAMMING

C165948 USB-C, native USB on GPIO18 / GPIO19, 5.1 k CC resistors, ESD part.

Sheet 2: ESP32-C3 core

ESP32-C3 CORE

ESP32-C3-MINI-1-N4 module, EN / BOOT buttons, status LEDs, decoupling, expansion header.

Sheet 3: Sensor ports (ultrasonic, float, probes, LDR, DHT11)

ULTRASONIC PORT

J2 header for JSN-SR04T / HC-SR04: +5V, TRIG, ECHO (5 V to 3.3 V divider), GND.

FLOAT SWITCH PORTS

J3: one dry-contact float switch input, with pull-up, series resistor, filter capacitor and ESD (the second float input was dropped to free GPIO10 for the DHT11).

PROBE PORT (COM + 5 LEVELS)

J4 + J8: COM (AC-coupled drive) and five level probes 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / FULL, each with a series resistor and a pull-down.

EXTRA SENSORS (LDR light, DHT11)

J11 2-pin terminal for an LDR (light sensor) with a 10 k divider into the ADC pin GPIO2, and J12 3-pin header for a DHT11 temperature / humidity sensor on GPIO10 with a 10 k pull-up.
